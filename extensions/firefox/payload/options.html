Before you accept, the extension can read grace’s local appearance settings to match its theme; this does not pair the browser or share browsing data. After you accept, this extension connects to grace. on this computer automatically at startup and reconnect. It shares a persistent browser-profile identifier, pairing authentication proofs, policy acknowledgements, and recurring connection checks. Pairing credentials are stored locally. Visited URLs stay in the browser. Browser or device details and error reports are not sent.

Declining or stopping sharing disconnects the companion. Protected sessions cannot start or continue without their required browser. Existing blocking rules can remain until a later authenticated release. Disabling or uninstalling this extension removes its protection.
